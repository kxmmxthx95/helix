# Character sprite credits

Body, head, hair, torso (shirt), legs (pants), and hat sprites in this folder
are cropped walk-cycle frames from the **Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC)** asset
collection, redistributed via the
[Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/sanderfrenken/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator)
(sanderfrenken), original art from
[LPC Revised Character Basics](https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-revised-character-basics)
by Eliza Wyatt (ElizaWy), Stephen Challener (Redshrike), Durrani, BenCreating,
Johannes Sjölund (Wulax), Thecilekli, and BlueCarrot16.
